About this project

Smart Classroom & AI Timetable Scheduler is a full-stack college management system powered by Supabase PostgreSQL and Vanilla JavaScript. It generates conflict-free weekly timetables, tracks live classroom availability, manages student attendance, and provides dedicated portals for Admins, Teachers, and Students.
